### Sabka common formula
1. **Audience = chhota vyapari / naya business shuru karne wala**, end customer nahi.
2. **Ek emotional Hindi headline** — chhota → bada, naukri → vyapar, tarakki, aatmanirbhar.
3. **Founder ka chehra** — trust ka sabse bada signal.
4. **Proof** — since year, "1 lakh+ businesses", "No.1", bada number.
5. **Products ki strip** — saree, kurti, suit, lehenga…
6. **Visual** — saree ke stack, showroom, khush dukandaar.
7. **Contact** — sirf ek ne phone number diya.

### Kesaria kahan jeet sakta hai (gaps)
- **Naam hi rang hai.** "केसरिया" ek rang hai, ek jazba hai — "केसरिया रंग चढ़ना" wala wordplay koi competitor copy nahi kar sakta. Kisi ka brand naam headline ka hissa nahi hai.
- **Asli awards.** Kisi competitor ke paas award proof nahi dikh raha. Kesaria ke paas 2026 ke 2 media awards hain.
- **Manufacturer ka fayda** (factory se seedha, bina bichauliye) kisi ne clearly nahi bola.
- **Rang alag.** Magenta, yellow, white pehle se liye hue hain — Kesaria apna **kesari + maroon + gold** own kare.
- **Mobile par text kat-ta hai** — Ishita ka address aur M.B. ka "YouTube Sumit business zone" mobile crop mein cut ho jaata hai. Hamara sab zaroori content safe area mein hai.

## 6. Designer brief — size & safe area

| Device | Kitna dikhta hai |
|---|---|
| Upload size | **2560 × 1440 px**, JPG/PNG, 6 MB se kam |
| TV | Pura 2560 × 1440 |
| Desktop | Beech ki patti 2560 × 423 |
| Tablet | 1855 × 423 |
| **Mobile (safe area)** | **1546 × 423 — headline, founder ka chehra, logo, phone sab yahin** |

- Headline mobile par bhi padhne layak ho: line 2 kam se kam ~100 px font (2560 canvas par).
- Mobile safe area ke bahar sirf decoration (saree stacks, store) — wahan koi zaroori text nahi.
- Bahut chhota address mat likho (Ishita House wali galti) — address "About" section mein daalo.
- Channel ke links (WhatsApp, Instagram, website) YouTube ke "Links" section mein bhi add karo.

## 7. Kya NAHI karna
- Bina proof "India's No.1" ya "1 lakh+ businesses" mat likho.
- 5–6 se zyada lines mat bharo — mobile par sab chhota ho jaata hai.
- Competitors ki line copy mat karo ("नाम तो सुना ही होगा", "बड़ा नौकर…") — Kesaria ka apna "केसरिया रंग" angle hi USP hai.
